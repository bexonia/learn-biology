# Skeletal Muscle Fiber & Sarcomere (`muscle_realistic.html`)

Interactive Cinema 3D PBR WebGL2 exploration of Phase 17B: a multinucleated skeletal myofiber with peripheral `H3K27ac` nuclei (`1AOI`), T-tubules, wrapped **Sarcoplasmic Reticulum** (`RyR1`, `SERCA1a` PDB `1SU4`), and a live sliding-filament **`2.2 µm ↔ 1.6 µm` Sarcomere** (`Myosin-II` S1 PDB `2MYS`, `F-Actin`–`Tropomyosin`–`Troponin` PDB `6KN8`, elastic `titin`), down to atomic `Ca²⁺` chelation and `Mg²⁺·ATP` hydrolysis (`0.1–0.5 nm`).

- **Live Contraction Toggle (`#modeBtn`):** `ca_trigger` (live cycle) ↔ `relaxed_22um` ↔ `contracted_16um`
