# Multipolar Neuron & Chemical Synapse (`neuron_realistic.html`)

Interactive Cinema 3D PBR WebGL2 exploration of Phase 17A: a complete human multipolar neuron from spiny dendrites and `H3K27ac` euchromatin soma nucleus through myelinated axon segments, saltatory **Nodes of Ranvier** (`Naᵥ1.7` PDB `6J8I`, `Kᵥ1.2` PDB `2R9R`, `Na⁺/K⁺`-ATPase PDB `3WGU`), and a **`20 nm` chemical synapse** (`SNARE` PDB `1SFC`, postsynaptic `AMPA` PDB `3KG2` & `nAChR` PDB `2BG9`), down to atomic neurotransmitters and ions (`0.1–0.5 nm`).

- **Views (`5`):** `overview`, `soma_epigenetics`, `node_ranvier`, `synapse`, `atoms`
- **Live Physiology Toggle (`#modeBtn`):** `action_potential` ↔ `resting` ↔ `synaptic_fire`
- **Inspectable Parts (`18`):** `dendrites`, `soma`, `nucleus_h3k27ac`, `axon_hillock`, `myelin_sheath`, `node_ranvier`, `nav17`, `kv12`, `nak_reset`, `axon_mt`, `terminal_bouton`, `cav_channel`, `synaptic_vesicle`, `snare_zipper`, `synaptic_cleft`, `ampa_receptor`, `nachr_receptor`, `neuro_atoms`
